# SportIn website: B2B copy audit (deck mockup, proto/deck.html)

Written by a B2B copywriter review agent, benchmarked against calendly.com and typeform.com.
Review only. Lines marked [confirm] need a real fact from the founder before they go live.

## 1. Diagnosis

1. The page talks about members, not to the buyer (marketing, community, partnerships, HR or
   commercial lead). No sentence names the buyer's goal (engagement, renewals, partner revenue,
   proof for the board) and how SportIn delivers it.
2. "What is it" stays unclear for too long: the abstract mission line appears three times (meta,
   hero, #what) and never says plainly "competitions, rewards and content in your app".
3. Order is mixed: Why/How/What is explained twice (#belief, #what, #ecosystem), formats are shown
   twice (#games repeats Knockout/Table, Conquer, boards), "who is it for" comes near the end, and
   "what does it cost me in effort" has no time, people or technical detail.
4. Almost no proof about SportIn: evidence numbers are third-party with their claim hidden in
   sr-only text; stories show headcounts but no outcome, quote, name or role; no logos.
5. Over-claims: "Engaged communities grow every number"; Bain's 95% is the top of a 25 to 95%
   range; "Connects to every wearable"; "keeps every competition fair"; "Each one walked 10,000
   steps a day"; "Live commentary for every match".
6. Rule breaks: "fan" in the hero rotator; "Champions League style", "national division style";
   "You don't need to be in the stadium"; League of Brands card scores in goals (2–1); "turned
   their kilometres into goals"; "unlock" x3; em dash in og:title.
7. Contradictions: Walking "five ways to rank" lists four; Running "up to the marathon" but screen
   stops at 15K and "ranked by pace" yet members choose; engine "Points turn into perks" vs step
   targets on Rewards; "1 users"; numbers "25.588" on screen vs "10,000" in copy.
8. Too few CTAs: only hero, engine, closing; none after formats, revenue, impact, how it works; no
   reassurance microcopy; closing says "Start with a pilot" but the button says "Book a demo"; hero
   secondary button jumps to #belief and skips the product.

## 2. Narrative spine: Play, share, earn, measure

1. Hero (#why), keep: outcome promise, one how-line, Book a demo + reassurance.
2. Who it's for (#who), move up right after the hero.
3. Problem (#problem), keep.
4. Evidence (#evidence), keep and fix (visible, honest claims + bridge line).
5. Spine (#what), repurpose the mission sentence into "Play, share, earn, measure".
6. PLAY, engine (#engine), keep.
7. PLAY, formats (#features), keep; de-football titles; CTA after the last page.
8. More formats (#games), cut or shrink to Live races, Challenges, Medals.
9. SHARE, member content (#ugc), keep, rewrite for the buyer.
10. EARN, ecosystem (#ecosystem), move directly before #revenue.
11. EARN, revenue (#revenue), keep + proof slot.
12. MEASURE, impact (#impact), keep + data-ownership line + CTA.
13. Proof, stories (#stories), move directly after impact.
14. Effort, how it works (#how-it-works), keep + timeline + what we need + "Plan a pilot".
15. Technology (#technology), move after how it works ("one partner, our own tech").
16. Belief (#belief), cut from the site flow (keep in present deck) or bridge after evidence.
17. Closing (#demo), keep; CTA "Plan your pilot".

CTAs: hero Book a demo + reassurance; end of engine (exists); end of formats "See it in your
brand"; after impact Book a demo; after how it works "Plan a pilot"; closing "Plan your pilot";
nav Book a demo.

## 6. What was applied (2026-10-23)

The founder approved this audit. Most sections it covers (hero, problem, evidence, spine, engine,
games, ecosystem, revenue, impact, stories, how it works, belief) were removed from the deck the same
day, so only these were applied to `proto/deck.html` and `proto/deck-jelly.html`:

- Hero (restored as page one later the same day): the proposed eyebrow, lede, outcome line,
  "See how it works" to #features, and the reassurance line without the call length. The rotator is
  member, customer, employee: the fan clip is gone and there is no volunteer clip yet.
- Meta: new description and og:description, og:title without the em dash.
- Intro page: lede without "unlock". The title "Three layers. Every game." stays.
- Formats 01 to 06: the proposed titles, ledes and kickers, the rail as Compete / Climb / Get
  rewarded, "1 users" fixed, Houses can carry a partner's name, Walking no longer claims five ways.
- Running: distances say 3K, 5K, 10K and 15K, which is what the screen shows, until the longest
  distance is confirmed. "Pace by default / Or rank by time or distance."
- Rewards: partner wording, every "unlock" removed, "A visit you can count" without the report claim.
- Member content: the proposed kicker, title and lede.
- Technology: the proposed eyebrow, title and lede. The station captions are fixed to one line, so
  the commentary caption is shortened to "Each match, written from real data." and Sportinho keeps
  its caption.
- Who it's for: the proposed lines for companies, brands, clubs and NGOs. The clubs line replaces
  "Fans feel part of the team, between every match". Gyms were not added, because the scene has one
  scroll step per audience.
- Closing: "Plan your pilot" and "No commitment. Or write to hello@sportin.io". The call length is
  left out until confirmed.
- Footer: Explore links point at the pages that exist; a "For organisations" list was added.

Not applied: every [confirm] item, the nav change (it pointed at removed pages), the "See it in your
brand" CTA inside the formats scene, the number format question, and the motion notes.
