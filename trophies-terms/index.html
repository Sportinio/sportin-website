Part A — Read First

A1. Plain-Language Summary

SportIn Trophies is a gamification feature. You walk. You earn a score. The score is displayed using euro units (e.g. "€12.50") because the earning rules are denominated that way — not because SportIn owes you €12.50. It is a number in an app. It is not money. You cannot withdraw it. You cannot spend it anywhere outside the SportIn Partner voucher network. During the beta, you cannot spend it at all. SportIn can cancel the entire programme tomorrow and owes you nothing.

A2. The Score Is Not Money

The Trophies Score is a loyalty score expressed in euro-denominated units. This is a design choice — it makes the earning rules intuitive — but it does not create any legal relationship between you and SportIn equivalent to a bank balance, a payment account, stored value, or electronic money.

Displaying "€12.50" in your Trophies wallet does not mean SportIn holds €12.50 on your behalf. SportIn does not hold any funds for you. SportIn does not owe you any amount. The euro sign is a unit of measure for your loyalty score, in the same way that airline programmes measure miles and hotel programmes measure points — the number has value only within the programme, only on SportIn's terms, and only if SportIn chooses to make redemption available.

No cash value — ever

Under no circumstances — not during beta, not post-beta, not on programme cancellation, not on account deletion — will SportIn pay out, transfer, or convert your Trophies Score into real euros, any other currency, bank credits, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or any other instrument of monetary value. There is no cash-out path. There will never be one.

A3. Beta Phase — Zero Obligation on SportIn

Beta: your score is currently unspendable

The programme is in beta. During the beta phase: (1) access is free — you pay nothing; (2) your Score accumulates but cannot be redeemed anywhere; (3) SportIn has not confirmed any Partner for voucher redemption; (4) SportIn is under no obligation to find Partners, activate redemption, or transition the programme out of beta; (5) SportIn may cancel the entire programme at any time, zeroing all Scores, without any obligation to compensate you in any form. By participating in the beta, you accept all of the above.

A4. Post-Beta — Subscription and Voucher Redemption

If and when SportIn transitions the programme out of beta, the following model is planned but not guaranteed:

  • Access will require a paid SportIn Pro subscription (currently planned at €99/month). You will not be charged anything during the beta phase.
  • If Partners are confirmed, subscribers will be able to use their accumulated Score to obtain vouchers from those Partners. The voucher system will be described in updated T&C, at that time.
  • If no Partners are confirmed, your Score continues to accumulate but remains unspendable. No refund of subscription fees will be given on the grounds that Partners are unavailable.
  • SportIn makes no commitment to confirm any Partners, activate voucher redemption, or maintain any particular partner network.

The specific terms governing subscriptions, voucher redemption, and any partner network will be set out in a separate addendum to these T&C, when and if that phase launches. Nothing in this document commits SportIn to launching that phase.

6.3 Turtles (negative Catches)

Turtles reduce your score

If your validated step count falls below the threshold for your current Level, you receive a Turtle — worth −€0.50 when sold. The more active you become (the higher your Level), the harder it is to avoid a Turtle on a rest day. An easy day actively costs you Score points. This is deliberate.

6.4 One Catch per day; missed days

One Catch per calendar day, per user. Days where step data is unavailable, fails fraud validation, or is identified as a spike (Section 13) produce no Catch — neither Puma nor Turtle. No retroactive Catch is awarded for missed days.

6.5 Values are Score units, not money

All Puma and Turtle values are loyalty score units denominated in euros for display purposes only. They are not financial obligations of SportIn. They may be changed at any time — see Section 12.

9. Selling Your Collection

9.1 Unsold Catches accumulate in your Collection. Medals and claimed Trophies credit your Score directly and are not part of the Collection.

9.2 Sell Collection. This action settles all unsold Catches simultaneously into a single net figure. All-or-nothing: you cannot partially sell or exclude individual items.

9.3 Final. Settlement is immediate and irreversible. The net result (positive or negative) is applied to your Score.

9.4 Value at time of sale. The value applied to each unsold Catch is the value assigned to that Catch type at the moment you press Sell Collection, not when you earned it. If SportIn has changed prices since you earned a Catch, the current price applies.

10. Negative Score

Your score can go below zero — this is not a debt

If you sell a Collection containing more Turtles than Pumas, your Score decreases and may go below zero. A negative Score is not a debt owed to SportIn. SportIn will never charge your payment method, withhold services, or take any adverse action to recover a negative Score. It simply means you have less (or no) voucher redemption capacity when that feature becomes available. A negative Score cannot go below zero at the point of voucher redemption — you simply have nothing to spend.

13. Fraud Prevention and Step Validation

13.1 All step data is subject to automated fraud validation. SportIn uses proprietary algorithms to detect abnormal patterns.

13.2 Spike zeroing. Days on which your step count is identified as a statistical spike inconsistent with your history will be zeroed — set to zero steps for Programme purposes. Zeroed days are visible in your Programme history. They produce no Catch.

13.3 Account suspension. Accounts exhibiting systematic fraud, GPS spoofing, step manipulation, or multi-account abuse will be suspended and all associated Scores zeroed without compensation. SportIn's determination is final.

13.4 Leaderboard integrity. Fraudulent accounts are excluded from the Medal leaderboard. SportIn may correct leaderboard rankings and Medal awards retroactively where fraud is discovered after the award.
