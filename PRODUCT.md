# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Decision makers at communities that move: brands with customer communities, sports clubs and fan clubs, gyms and fitness chains, companies and teams, charities and cities. They arrive from a referral, a deck or a search, often on a laptop between meetings, and want to understand in seconds what SportIn does for their community and whether it is worth a demo.

## Product Purpose
SportIn turns communities into active ecosystems that generate new revenue and measurable impact by gamifying real-world movement. The website's job is to make that clear fast, prove it with the real product and real people, and get the visitor to book a demo. Success is a booked demo from the right kind of community.

## Brand Personality
Energetic, human, premium. Movement and real people carry the energy; the finish is calm and high-end, like Calendly, Typeform and Apple product pages. The voice is plain and confident: specific claims, short sentences, no hype words. Headline: "Give every member a part to play."

## Anti-references
- Glow of any kind (coloured halos, neon, blurred brand-colour shadows); one tiny neutral shadow at most.
- Generic AI landing-page grammar: identical icon-title-text card grids, eyebrow kickers on every section, hero-metric templates, gradient text.
- Football-only framing: no fan, match, stadium or fixture words in headlines; clubs are one community among many.
- Characters or people drawn in code; people are real photos and footage or professional work.
- Fake numbers presented as real; demo figures are labelled.

## Design Principles
- Show the product, not a diagram of it: the real SportIn app and real communities on every key screen.
- One idea per screen, one signature motion per section; everything else stays quiet.
- Community first: speak to every kind of community, with real people and real results.
- Earn trust with honesty: verified numbers only, demo data labelled.
- Premium restraint: generous space, Archivo set wide for titles, Instrument Sans for text, SportIn blue as the one accent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in light and dark mode. Every animation has a reduced-motion alternative that shows the final state. Keyboard focus is always visible. Content works at 390px wide without sideways scroll.
