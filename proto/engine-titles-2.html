Engine and integration titles: round 2

Three new directions. Instead of tuning the centred title, each one changes how the text and the 3D scene share the page. Each shows Competition, Leaderboard, Integration and the finished engine. Mockup only, not in the deck.

Version A: Verbs as the index

Idea: Four verbs stacked on the left: Compete, Climb, Claim, Connect. The one on screen is ink, the rest are pale, so the title is also the progress. The scene moves right, like 11 Who it's for and 12 Delivery.

Trade-off: The page reads like one sentence and shows where you are. The scene gets about 60% of the width.

Version B: Subtitles

Idea: No title over the scene. One quiet line at the bottom, like film subtitles: a 4-part progress bar, the step name and one sentence. The big title only opens and closes the section.

Trade-off: The scene gets the whole page and the text never competes with it. The least "designed": the plate does the talking.

Version C: One big word

Idea: The step's verb, very large and very pale, sits behind the scene: Compete, Climb, Connect. One small line in front, at the bottom. The 04 row stays top left.

Trade-off: The most memorable, with a poster feel. The verb has to stay pale, or it fights the plate.
