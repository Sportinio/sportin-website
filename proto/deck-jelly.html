Why

Every move brings us closer.

Belonging shouldn't have to wait for the next event. People already walk, run and move every day. That movement can bring them closer to the clubs, brands and causes they care about.

How

Movement becomes a way to take part.

A morning walk can score for a team. A run can claim territory on a map, climb a leaderboard or move a whole community toward a shared goal. You don't need to be in the stadium to play a part.

New revenue

Participation becomes revenue.

When people show up every day, partners want to be part of it. The game creates new places to sell.

  • Sponsored leagues and seasons

    A partner's name on the league, the season or the trophy, seen by every member, every day.

  • Partners who found a House

    Brands found a House on the map. Members run under its colours and claim territory in its name.

  • Brand-funded rewards

    Partners fund the prizes members play for, and meet people at the moment they earn them.

  • Premium seasons and team entries

    Members and teams pay to enter the seasons they care about most.
