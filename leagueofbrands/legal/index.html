Section 01

Overview & How You Accept

League of Brands is a season-based movement competition operated by SportIn.io SRL through the SportIn platform — the mobile apps, the web apps at app.sportin.io and try.sportin.io, and the website at sportin.io. Teams compete in movement-based challenges (steps, distance and activity) tracked through the app and scored on the leaderboard.

This Legal Agreement gathers, in one place and in plain language, everything that applies to you as a member of a participating team: how the competition works, the health declaration you must make, how your personal data is handled, the rules of the app, fair-play conduct, prizes, and the limits of SportIn's responsibility. It reflects SportIn's full terms (version 3.0, 2026), including its Privacy Policy, End User Licence, Health Disclaimer, Prize Policy and Acceptable Use Policy.

There is nothing to sign
This is not a contract you sign. Before your first competition window, you simply review this agreement and then confirm inside the SportIn app — by ticking the acknowledgment checkbox — that you have read and understood it. That in-app confirmation is your acceptance. No signature, stamp or paperwork is required.

1.1 How participation works

  • Your team is enrolled in League of Brands and you join it on the SportIn platform as a player.
  • Once you are registered, your activity data is tracked during each competition window and counts toward your team's score.
  • SportIn operates the platform, runs the competition, displays results, and keeps the platform secure.
  • Taking part is entirely voluntary. You are never required to participate, and you can stop at any time.

1.2 Who can take part

  • The minimum age to take part in competition tracking that involves health or location data is 18 years.
  • Where national law allows participation from age 16 for online services that do not involve health data, a lower threshold may apply — but no one under 18 may take part in health-data tracking without verified parental or guardian consent.
  • The platform is not directed at anyone under 16, and SportIn does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.
Accepting the Terms & Privacy Policy
By confirming in the app, you also accept SportIn's Terms of Service (sportin.io/terms) and Privacy Policy (sportin.io/privacy-policy), which form part of this agreement.
Section 02

Health Disclaimer & Self-Declaration

Important — please read carefully
League of Brands involves sustained physical activity over a competition window of up to 24 hours. Before your first competition, you must read this Health Disclaimer and complete the self-declaration at the end of this section (the in-app checkbox). Participation without completing it is not permitted.

2.1 Nature of the competition

The competition tracks physical movement — steps, distance and GPS-verified activity — over a window of up to 24 hours. You may walk, run, cycle or do other physical activity in any location. There is no minimum or maximum activity requirement; what you do is entirely voluntary and self-directed.

2.2 SportIn is not a medical or health service

SportIn is a technology platform. The platform, the competition and any Movement Index (MI) score are not medical advice, clinical recommendations, coaching programmes or therapeutic services. SportIn does not assess your individual fitness level, medical conditions or physical capacity, and does not replace professional medical advice.

2.3 Your responsibility

  • Physical activity carries inherent risks — including, but not limited to, musculoskeletal injury, cardiovascular events, accidents while exercising outdoors (including traffic accidents), dehydration and the worsening of pre-existing conditions.
  • You take part entirely at your own risk.
  • SportIn is not liable for any injury, illness, accident, death or health consequence arising from or connected with participation — whether or not you completed this declaration.

2.4 Medical clearance recommendation

SportIn strongly recommends you consult a licensed medical professional before taking part if you:

  • have a known cardiovascular condition, heart disease or irregular heart rhythm;
  • have diabetes, asthma or any chronic respiratory condition;
  • have a musculoskeletal injury or have had recent surgery;
  • are pregnant or postpartum;
  • have not done regular physical activity for more than three months;
  • have any other condition that may be affected by sustained physical activity.

This recommendation is advisory rather than mandatory, but SportIn strongly encourages you to follow it.

2.5 Outdoor activity & insurance

  • If you exercise outdoors (running, walking, cycling), you do so entirely at your own risk and must follow all traffic laws, safety regulations and local rules. SportIn is not responsible for accidents or incidents in public spaces.
  • SportIn does not provide any health, accident or liability insurance. You are advised to ensure you have appropriate personal insurance before taking part.
Mandatory Self-Declaration
What you confirm in the app before your first competition
  • I have read and understood this Health Disclaimer in full.
  • I am voluntarily choosing to take part in the competition.
  • I am physically fit for sustained physical activity, or a medical professional has confirmed I may take part.
  • I have no known medical condition that would make up to 24 hours of self-directed physical activity unsafe for me, or I have taken appropriate professional advice about my condition.
  • I accept full personal responsibility for any health event, injury, accident or adverse outcome before, during or after my participation.
  • I understand SportIn is a technology platform, not a medical or health service, and does not assess my individual fitness or medical suitability.
  • I understand that data from my wearable or phone may not always perfectly reflect my activity, and I accept competition results based on the recorded data.
  • I am 18 or older, or I have provided verified parental/guardian consent if I am 16–17 in a jurisdiction that permits it.
Section 03

Your Data & Privacy

SportIn handles your personal data in line with the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and Romanian Law no. 190/2018. SportIn.io SRL is the data controller. You can reach the team and the Data Protection Officer at privacy@sportin.io.

3.1 What data is collected

Only the minimum data needed to run the service is collected:

3.2 What SportIn does NOT do with your data

  • Does not sell your data — never has, never will.
  • Does not use your data for advertising — no personalised ads, no ad networks.
  • Does not share your data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Does not make automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
  • Does not use your data for anything beyond operating the platform and the competition.

3.3 Who receives your data

  • Cloud infrastructure providers — acting as processors under a data processing agreement, with data stored inside the EEA.
  • Third-party integrations (such as Spike) — only to relay your activity data to the platform, under their own terms.
  • Your team — only anonymised, aggregate team statistics, unless you explicitly consent to share your individual results.
  • Law enforcement — only when required by law or a binding legal order.

Personal data is not transferred outside the EEA.

3.4 Your rights (GDPR Articles 15–22)

  • Access — get a copy of your data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — delete your account instantly via Settings › Account › Delete Account.
  • Restriction — restrict processing while data is being verified.
  • Portability — receive your data in JSON format.
  • Object — object to legitimate-interest processing.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw any consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.
  • Complaint — file a complaint with ANSPDCP (anspdcp.ro) or your local EU supervisory authority.

Requests are answered within 30 days, extendable by 60 days for complex cases.

3.5 Account deletion & inactive accounts

  • Instant deletion: press delete in Settings › Account › Delete Account and your profile, competition history, health data and all associated personal data are immediately and irreversibly deleted.
  • Legal exception: limited pseudonymised records may be kept for up to 36 months where Romanian fiscal or commercial law requires it.
  • Inactive accounts: if an account is not accessed for 12 continuous months, SportIn emails you at least 30 days before deletion. If you do not log in or respond within that 30-day window, the account and its data are permanently deleted. Log in at least once every 12 months to keep your account.

3.6 Security, cookies & changes

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256); passwords hashed (bcrypt/Argon2) and never stored in plaintext; role-based access with least privilege; regular security assessments; data hosted in the EEA.
  • Only strictly necessary cookies are used (session, authentication, security). No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • You will be notified of material changes by email or in-app at least 14 days before they take effect. Where consent is the legal basis, a new consent record is taken.

Section 05

Fair Play & Acceptable Use

5.1 Prohibited conduct

To keep the competition fair for everyone, you must not:

  • Create fake accounts, impersonate others or fabricate activity data.
  • Use bots, automation, GPS spoofing, treadmill manipulation or any method to artificially inflate your metrics.
  • Share your account credentials or allow unauthorised access.
  • Post hateful, discriminatory, sexually explicit or unlawful content.
  • Reverse-engineer, decompile or scrape the platform.
  • Use the platform for unauthorised commercial purposes.
  • Interfere with the platform's security, integrity or availability.
  • Try to access other people's accounts or restricted areas.
  • Publish, distribute or commercially exploit the individual results or step counts of other players without their consent.

5.2 Anti-cheating

SafeGuard AI
SportIn runs SafeGuard AI, an automated monitoring system. Suspicious activity may trigger a review and action. Manually entered steps and simulated movement are detected and excluded.

5.3 Consequences & appeals

  • Breaking these rules may lead to a warning, temporary suspension, disqualification and forfeiture of prizes, permanent termination, or referral to legal authorities.
  • SportIn gives notice where practical, except where immediate action is needed for safety, legal or integrity reasons.
  • Appeals: you may appeal a disqualification decision to hello@sportin.io within 14 days. Appeals are reviewed within 30 days, and SportIn's decision on appeal is final.
  • Decisions taken under SportIn's discretionary rights over the competition (Section 08) are not subject to appeal.
  • Report any violations confidentially to hello@sportin.io.
Section 06

Prizes

No prizes by default
A competition includes prizes only where a separate prize schedule has been agreed in writing. If there is no prize schedule, no prizes are offered, promised or owed. The points below apply only where a prize schedule exists.

6.1 Eligibility

To be eligible for a prize, a winner must:

  • be a registered player who took part in the relevant competition;
  • have complied with this agreement, the Terms of Service and the fair-play rules throughout;
  • not have been disqualified, suspended or banned;
  • have completed the Health Self-Declaration before participating;
  • provide valid identification and fiscal data if requested.

6.2 Delivery, changes & forfeiture

  • Prizes are delivered no earlier than one month and no later than six months after the competition ends. SportIn decides the delivery method and timing within that window.
  • Before delivery, SportIn may, at its discretion, cancel a prize, substitute one of comparable or lesser value, or withhold a prize for rule violations or unverified eligibility. No compensation is owed for cancelled or substituted prizes.
  • Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred unless the prize schedule says so.
  • Unclaimed prizes (no claim within 30 days of notification, or no fiscal data provided in that time) are forfeited.

6.3 Tax

The winner is responsible for all tax on a prize. Under Romanian fiscal law, prizes over 600 RON are subject to 10% income tax plus applicable social contributions (CASS). SportIn withholds and remits taxes where legally required and issues the appropriate fiscal documents.
