  1. Every move counts.

    Walks, runs, rides and gym sessions all earn points, synced from the phone and the watch members already use. The commute and the lunch walk now count for your gym.

  2. Every member, on a board.

    Weekly leagues by member, by class and by time slot. The 6am crew against the lunch crowd, with something to play for every day of the week.

  3. Rewards they move for.

    A free PT session, a shake from the bar, a month on the house. You and your partners set the rewards; members unlock them by staying active.

  4. A crew to show up for.

    Teams, streaks and a feed of each other’s wins. Skipping a session means letting the crew down, which is the best reason there is to come in.

See what happens outside your walls.

Your dashboard shows who is active between visits, who is drifting, and which games bring people back, so you can act before a member cancels.

  • Keep members longer

    Members who play have a reason to come back every week, and you see the ones slipping before they cancel.

  • Grow through your members

    Invites and team challenges turn active members into the people who bring their friends.

  • Open new revenue

    Partner brands fund rewards and sponsor seasons. Premium challenges can be sold as add-ons.
