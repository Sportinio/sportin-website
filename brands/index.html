The fairness engine

A 5-level system so walkers and runners compete fairly

Participants are placed into 5 levels based on their average steps across the 30 days. The key is how we measure progress: a logarithmic Movement Index where improvements at lower step counts count for more.

That means a colleague going from 3,000 to 6,000 steps is rewarded just as meaningfully as a marathoner adding a few thousand more. Nobody is unfairly compared to the office runner — every fitness level has a real shot at winning its tier. This is usually the first question HR asks, and it's built into the core.
